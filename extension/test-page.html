Test Banner Detection

Click a button to spawn a simulated cookie banner. The extension should detect and close it automatically.

What Should Happen

  1. You click a "Spawn Banner" button above
  2. A cookie banner appears at the bottom of the page
  3. BannerBanner extension detects it (usually within 1 second)
  4. Extension clicks the appropriate button based on your settings:
    • Necessary Only → Clicks "Reject All"
    • All Cookies → Clicks "Accept All"
    • Custom → Clicks "Settings" and adjusts toggles
  5. Banner disappears
  6. 🍌 Banana celebration appears (if enabled)!
