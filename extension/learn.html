Learn with the Bananers

Your popup-dismissing companions: who they are, what they've learned, and how they take popups apart.

👋 Welcome to Banana Town! Bananers are learning companions that investigate and dismiss annoying popups. They only work on sites you invite them to: open the toolbar popup on any site and click "Deploy a bananer here". Every popup they dismiss becomes a memory you can browse and replay below.

Meet your bananers

Each bananer specializes in one kind of popup and earns XP for every fingerprint learned and memory recalled.

Learned popup memories

Every dismissed popup is fingerprinted so bananers can suppress it before it even paints next time. Replay any takedown to see how it was done.
